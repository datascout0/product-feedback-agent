This document summarizes the current product strategy for internal planning
purposes. It is intended to give the analysis agent grounding context when
weighing customer feedback against stated priorities.

# Mission

Help small and mid-size teams turn scattered customer feedback into a small
number of well-evidenced product opportunities, without replacing human
judgment on what actually ships.

# Strategic Pillars

## Trustworthy Evidence

Every recommendation the agent makes must be traceable back to specific,
quoted customer feedback. Unsupported claims are treated as defects.

## Bounded Autonomy

The agent proposes and ranks opportunities. It does not decide the roadmap.
Humans remain the approval boundary for anything that changes the product.

# Non-Goals

- Automatically prioritizing the roadmap
- Generating solutions or experiments on the agent's behalf
- Replacing qualitative customer research
